MyMoney

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 · 100% Free & Offline Personal Finance App

1. Overview

MyMoney is a personal expense tracker, budget planner, and wealth manager designed with strict privacy by default. Your financial data belongs exclusively to you and remains entirely on your own device.

2. Data Collection & Storage

  • Zero Personal Data Collected: MyMoney does not collect, transmit, sell, or store your financial transactions, account balances, budgets, savings goals, profile details, or optional PINs on any external server.
  • 100% On-Device Storage: All profiles and ledger records are stored locally inside your device's private application storage.
  • No Ads & No Tracking SDKs: MyMoney contains zero third-party advertising networks, behavioral trackers, or analytics telemetry.

3. Permissions & User-Initiated Exports

  • Local Storage / Downloads: Only used when you explicitly tap Export CSV or JSON Backup to save a copy of your ledger into your device's Downloads folder.
  • File Picker: Only used when you explicitly choose to import a JSON backup file from your device.

4. Data Deletion & Control

You have complete control over your data at all times. You can delete individual transactions, wipe all sample data, delete any saved profile, or permanently erase all application data directly inside the app's Settings screen or by uninstalling the app from your Android device.

5. Children's Privacy

MyMoney is a general-audience personal finance utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
